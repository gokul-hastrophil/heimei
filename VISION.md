# Heimei — Vision

## Purpose

Why Heimei exists, stated once, canonically. Every other governance document exists to serve this — none of them should restate it.

## Scope

The lifelong mission, independent of what's actually built today. For current status, see `PROJECT.md`. For the principles that govern *how* this gets built, see `CONSTITUTION.md`. For the staged path from here to there, see `ROADMAP.md`.

## What Heimei is

Heimei is not an operating system — Ubuntu remains that. Heimei is the intelligence layer above it: the system that manages knowledge, intelligence, memory, reasoning, and digital capability, the way an operating system manages hardware.

## The ultimate goal

A trustworthy **Digital Twin**. Not a chatbot. Not an assistant. Not an automation framework.

A Digital Twin that grows alongside its human for decades, continuously learning:

- how its human thinks
- how decisions are made
- engineering style and software architecture philosophy
- product philosophy
- business thinking
- creativity
- research habits
- communication style
- priorities, values, and long-term goals

— and preserves that understanding permanently, not as a cache that can be cleared, but as a lasting record that outlives any one session, tool, or model.

## The digital human model

Heimei should eventually possess digital equivalents of human capability, not as a gimmick but as a literal design target for what it needs to be able to do:

| Human capability | Digital equivalent |
|---|---|
| Eyes | See the desktop, browser, IDE, camera, documents, images, video |
| Ears | Listen to meetings, speech, voice notes, video |
| Voice | Speak naturally — teach, present, explain |
| Hands | Operate the keyboard, mouse, terminal, browser, cloud, IDE, applications |
| Memory | Never forget |
| Knowledge | Understand |
| Reasoning | Plan, research, challenge assumptions |
| Creativity | Invent, design, build |
| Business sense | Discover opportunities, create value, operate products |

## Long-term objectives

Heimei should eventually understand every project, every repository, every machine, every company, every business, every document, every conversation, every decision, every failure, and every success it has touched — and continuously learn, improve, research, reason, organize, remember, connect, and plan.

## Business vision

Heimei should help create value: discovering problems worth solving, market opportunities, product ideas, engineering improvements, research opportunities, automation opportunities, startup ideas — and turning ideas into executable plans. Eventually, helping operate businesses, products, and digital assets.

**Revenue is not the objective. Creating value is the objective. Revenue follows value.**

## Human control

Heimei must never become uncontrollable. Significant actions require explicit approval unless the owner has deliberately delegated that authority. Heimei may observe, learn, research, prepare, recommend, plan, and automate delegated tasks — but every recommendation must be transparent: why, based on what evidence, and what alternatives exist.
